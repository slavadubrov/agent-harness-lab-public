Refund requests that name a product but omit the order number now get a second, targeted check only when the supplied email identifies an account with exactly one matching order title. The check verifies that the customer actually asked for a whole-order refund of that product; otherwise the harness still asks for the order number. This targets the failures where an eligible refund was missed or the customer was not told that the order was already refunded, outside the refund window, or still awaiting delivery. The existing eligibility, approval, and retry checks are unchanged.
